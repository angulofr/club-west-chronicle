# Club West Chronicle

Interactive historical chronicle, HOA governance vault, litigation docket tracker, and 2026 redevelopment comparator for the **162-acre Club West Golf Course** in Ahwatukee Foothills (Phoenix, Arizona).

## What It Covers
- **Ownership & Valuation Flow (`1989–2026`):** Shea Homes → Wilson Gee (Kwang Co. / Hiro Investment) → The Edge at Club West LLC (`$750,000`, June 2020) → Community Harmony Coalition LLC (`$2.1M`, May 29, 2026) → Nathan & Associates listing → competing 2026 builder pre-applications.
- **2026 Builder Pre-Application Comparator:** Side-by-side acreage and density comparison between **D.R. Horton's 573-home concept** and **Sekisui House Ltd.'s November 2026 proposal** (`278 single-family homes` and `88.5 acres` of amenitized/passive open space and drainage).
- **HOA & Court Dockets Vault:** 1989 Master CC&Rs (`75%` supermajority rule), 1993 Golf Course Declaration, 2010 Assignment of Declarant Rights, 2018–2019 executive-session amendments, `CV2020-003577` (Judge Daniel J. Kiley), `CV2021-018435` (Judge Susanna C. Pineda), and `1 CA-SA 25-0117` (Arizona Court of Appeals, Oct 27, 2025).
- **Stakeholder Directory & Verified Source Archive:** Direct links to *Ahwatukee Foothills News*, 360 Community Management's Foothills Club West portal, and court opinions.
